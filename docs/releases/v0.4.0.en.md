# v0.4.0 — public storefront and cart

- Product-led home page with search, illustrated categories, a compact product grid and working collections; responsive product, seller, saved-product and order pages.
- Guest browsing only for explicitly opted-in published products. Existing listings remain private after migration. Drafts, private variants and their photos stay private; seller eligibility and revocation still apply.
- Multi-seller cart, quantity updates, totals, price reconfirmation, atomic checkout and duplicate protection. Simulated orders do not change real inventory or take payments.
- A separate reproducible import command adds 24 demo products, 48 variants, 6 categories and 3 fictional stores. Licensed photos include credits. No preset passwords or fabricated reviews.
- Ordinary installations remain empty; demo ordering remains disabled by default.

[Installation](../../README.en.md) · [Demo storefront](../runbooks/storefront-demo-en.md) · [Image credits](../demo-image-credits.md) · [Release verification](v0.4.0-verification.md).

Back up the database, photos and original `.env` before upgrading, then apply migrations. Owners explicitly opt old listings into public visibility. Rollback requires the earlier code/image and matching saved data; do not delete volumes or regenerate keys for an existing database.

This is a locally runnable project, not an internet deployment. Real payment and shipping integrations are absent. Code-use terms are unchanged; third-party image licenses are listed separately.
